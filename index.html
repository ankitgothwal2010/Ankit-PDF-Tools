Are my uploaded PDF documents private and secure?

Yes, 100%! Unlike traditional PDF websites that upload your files to remote cloud servers, Ankit PDF Tools processes your files directly inside your browser sandbox using WebAssembly and client-side JavaScript. Your confidential files never touch our servers.

Is the ₹19 Premium really a one-time payment?

Yes! There are no recurring monthly charges, automatic bank deductions, or hidden fees. You pay ₹19 once via UPI QR, submit your 12-digit transaction reference (UTR), and unlock lifetime access.

How does UPI payment verification work?

You scan our genuine UPI QR code using any UPI-compliant app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Cred) and pay ₹19. Enter the 12-digit UTR/Reference number provided by your banking app. Your payment is recorded and verified, granting your account lifetime premium status. You can restore your purchase on other devices using your UTR.

Does this website work on iPhone and mobile phones?

Yes! Ankit PDF Tools is fully responsive and optimized for touch interactions on mobile phones, iPads, iPhones (Safari/Chrome), Android devices, and desktop browsers.

Privacy Policy for Ankit PDF Tools

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Information We Collect: Ankit PDF Tools does not require account creation for free features. When you purchase Premium, we collect your payment reference number (UTR/Transaction ID) solely to verify your one-time ₹19 transaction.

2. How Uploaded Documents Are Handled: Your documents are processed locally within your web browser using HTML5 Canvas, PDF.js, and client-side WebAssembly. No PDF or image file contents are uploaded to or stored on remote web servers.

3. Browser-Side Execution vs Server: All file conversions and PDF transformations run on client hardware. For complex conversions (e.g. DOCX/OCR simulation), processing is completed in your local browser sandbox.

4. Payment Information: We do not ask for or collect UPI PINs, credit card numbers, OTPs, or net banking passwords. Payments occur entirely inside your authorized UPI banking app.

5. Cookies and Local Storage: We use browser localStorage solely to retain your remaining daily free operations count and your verified Premium entitlement token across browser sessions.
